# Limitations

1. **Retrospective selection:** The best recent-720 specification was selected after observing 2026 development results.
2. **Pregame timestamps:** Historical official lineups are reconstructed from final game pages rather than a contemporaneously archived lineup-confirmation feed.
3. **Post-starter runs:** Official pitcher responsibility runs differ from every run physically scored after the starter left the game.
4. **Unavailable external information:** Market odds, weather, verified injury status, travel, and exact bullpen availability are not primary inputs.
5. **League-specific data:** Findings are based on KBO seasons and may not transfer directly to other leagues.
6. **Data redistribution:** Complete official raw data is not distributed in this public repository.
7. **Final confirmation:** A prospective immutable ledger is still required.
