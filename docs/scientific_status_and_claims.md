# Scientific status and allowed claims

## Allowed claims

- Official multi-season records and strict-prior player summaries contain predictive signal.
- A clean start-only starter block improved the tested team baseline.
- The V11.1 official-event batter block added incremental value in the V12 development evaluation.
- Strongly regulated logistic regression was more stable than the tested complex families under the temporal protocol.
- The tested player-level relief index did not add value and was rejected.
- Identity and role-scope audits materially changed the research conclusions.

## Claims requiring qualification

- 2026 predictions contain no target-game or same-date leakage, **but** 2026 was used for method comparison.
- The recent-720 model is the best observed development model, **not** an untouched final champion.
- The CV-selected `C=0.03` model is cleaner scientifically, but its 2026 evaluation is still part of an already observed period.
- Bootstrap intervals quantify uncertainty under date-cluster resampling; they do not remove model-selection uncertainty.

## Prohibited claims

- “The model has been proven to generalize to future KBO seasons.”
- “2026 was a completely untouched test.”
- “Relief pitching is unimportant.”
- “The composite score measures economic player value.”
- “Complex models are universally worse than logistic regression.”
- “No data leakage exists” without distinguishing feature leakage from test-set reuse.

## Required next evidence

The final generalization claim requires predictions written before first pitch to an immutable ledger, with model hashes, feature hashes, source cutoff timestamps, lineup/starter snapshot hashes, and separate postgame outcome settlement.
