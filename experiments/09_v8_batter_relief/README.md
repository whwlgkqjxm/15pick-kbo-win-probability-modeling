# V8 — Initial batter and relief negative results

## Hypothesis

Do initial batter and player-level relief indices improve the V7 structure?

## Data and protocol

Official-event batter and relief scores, prior methods, pools, and audits.

The experiment follows the date-level strict-prior contract applicable at that stage. Protocol differences are not hidden; absolute metrics from different protocols are not ranked as if they were one common leaderboard.

## Result

Batter addition and relief addition worsened the 2025 stack; relief standalone was null.

## Decision

Reject both from the primary model; retain evidence and limitations.

## What changed next

A failed representation was not generalized into a failed domain.
