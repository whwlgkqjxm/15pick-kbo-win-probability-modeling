# Multi-season official data foundation

## Hypothesis

Can the study create a complete, identity-safe, strict-prior 2024–2026 foundation?

## Data and protocol

1,864 completed games; 65,554 player-game occurrences.

The experiment follows the date-level strict-prior contract applicable at that stage. Protocol differences are not hidden; absolute metrics from different protocols are not ranked as if they were one common leaderboard.

## Result

65,554/65,554 identities resolved; zero name-only forced merges; zero temporal violations.

## Decision

Freeze canonical data, identity, scoring parity, and history; do not recollect without cause.

## What changed next

Data quality and provenance were treated as model prerequisites.
